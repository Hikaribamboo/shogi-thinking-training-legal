Content Policy

Shogi Next Move Trainer / Last Updated: May 1, 2026

1. About This Policy

This policy explains how the shogi puzzles, answer choices, explanations, position evaluations, and other content provided in this app are created.

2. Puzzle Creation Policy

The puzzles in this app are created based on positions that the developer encountered while studying shogi positions with the help of a shogi engine. Positions are selected when they raise interesting questions, appear educational, or seem suitable for practicing next-move thinking.

The purpose of the puzzles is not only to guess the correct move, but also to help users learn how to think about positions, compare candidate moves, and improve their reading process.

3. Use of Shogi Engines

In the process of creating and reviewing puzzles, this app refers to shogi engine analysis, evaluation scores, candidate moves, and related information.

However, engine analysis is used as reference information to support understanding of a position. It does not guarantee a single absolute correct answer in every position.

4. Explanations

The explanations are created based on the developer's shogi knowledge as an amateur 5-dan player, together with shogi engine analysis. The app aims to provide explanations with a reasonable level of reliability.

However, shogi is an extremely complex game, and there may be multiple ways to interpret a position or evaluate candidate moves. Therefore, the app does not guarantee that every explanation is completely correct or that it covers every possible strong idea.

5. Correct Moves and Evaluations

In this app, the move considered most suitable for learning purposes is set as the correct move.

Depending on the position, there may be other strong candidate moves. Also, the best move or evaluation may change depending on engine depth, analysis conditions, and interpretation of the position.

6. Relationship with External Content

The puzzles in this app are created based on independently studied, edited, and reviewed content.

Even when actual game records, publicly available positions, books, websites, or other materials are used as references, the app does not aim to reproduce them as-is. The content is reconstructed, edited, and reviewed as learning material before being provided in the app.

7. Corrections and Improvements

If errors or points for improvement are found in puzzles, correct moves, explanations, evaluation information, or other content, the content may be reviewed and, if necessary, corrected, replaced, or removed.
